ISO New England’s summer intern class of 2026 presented highlights of their work and accomplishments at a poster expo held Aug. 12 at the ISO’s campus in Holyoke, Massachusetts. The fourth annual showcase attracted a crowd of employees, who listened to interns review their posters and asked questions about the projects they worked on this summer.
